About Us

About Our Work

Al Shahiq Carpentry is an Interior Design company based in the UAE that has built up a reputation around customers based upon reliability, consistency of delivery and value. We offer comprehensive interior design, creating beautifully crafted interiors for our clients.

With over 25 years of experience, Al Shahiq Carpentry has completed more than 1000 projects. We have worked with commercial offices, retail units, supermarkets, hotels, saloon, restaurants, schools, healthcare and residential projects in Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah & Ajman. We are experienced in the planning, design, and delivery of all types of interior design projects. Whether you are renovating your villa or commercial space our interior design firm is a one-stop solution for interior consultancy and design, our team of interior designers and project managers are committed to delivering your project with full satisfactions.
